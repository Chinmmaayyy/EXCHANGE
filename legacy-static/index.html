About the Coach

Amar Ravindra More

Chess Coach, Exchange Chess Academy

Amar Ravindra More is a FIDE-rated chess coach based in Mumbai, with 25+ years of experience teaching chess to children and adults. His coaching is built around patient, structured, one-to-one attention — helping every student progress at a pace suited to them, whether they are picking up the pieces for the first time or preparing seriously for tournament play.

Over the years, he has taught chess at Sharda Mandir School, Gamdevi and Arya Vidya Mandir School, Bandra Kurla Complex, alongside personal 1-to-1 coaching, small group sessions and home visits across Mumbai.

Chess Coaching Programs

Structured Chess Coaching for Every Level

Coaching is designed around each student's current level and goals, not a fixed one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Programme A

Chess Fundamentals

For absolute beginners — learning the rules, board understanding, piece movement, basic tactics and checkmate patterns.

Best for: New learners & young beginners

Programme B

Intermediate Chess Development

Tactics, strategy and calculation, along with openings, middlegame understanding and endgame technique.

Best for: Students building beyond the basics

Programme C

Advanced & Tournament Training

Advanced calculation, strategic planning, tournament preparation, game analysis and practical play.

Best for: Serious & tournament-bound players

Programme D

Personal 1-to-1 Coaching

Fully customized coaching based on the student's current level, learning goals and pace of progress.

Best for: Focused, individual attention

Programme E

Small Group Coaching

Guided coaching for small groups of 2–3 students, combining focused attention with peer learning.

Best for: Siblings & friends learning together
